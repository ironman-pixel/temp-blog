press `Win + R` type `services.mac`

- Look for a service named something like `MySQL`, `MariaDB`, or `MariaDB_Service`.
- Right-click on the service and select **"Stop"**.
- To prevent it from starting automatically on reboot, you can right-click, go to **"Properties"**, and change **"Startup type"** to **"Manual"** or **"Disabled"**.
